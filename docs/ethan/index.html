Three terms you need

Supply ÷ demand

Radiologist capacity divided by the work that needs doing. Below 1 is a shortage (today ≈ ); above 1 is a surplus. Charts measure both against 2026 demand = 1.

Meaningful oversupply

Supply ÷ demand above 1.10: at least 10% more radiologists than the work needs. The threshold is a convention for a clearly noticeable surplus; past gluts, when new graduates struggled to find jobs, were never measured on this scale.

AI-progress regimes

Each future is Stall (15%), Trend (55%), Fast (18%) or Transformative (12%: AI eventually does nearly all a radiologist's cognitive work). Most of the downside sits in the last one. More →

The short version for you

Your first job market (2035) is probably not oversupplied. The chance of a meaningful shortage then () is about the same as the chance of meaningful oversupply (). Most of the radiologists you will compete with are already in training, and imaging demand keeps rising with an older population.

About half of the 2035 risk is one scenario. If AI becomes transformative (about of futures), oversupply by 2035 is close to certain. In the other futures it is . So the most useful thing you can do is watch for the early signs of that scenario (below) before you commit.

In surplus futures, the usual pattern is fewer openings for new graduates and flatter pay rather than layoffs, which favors people who are already established by then. Read the exact numbers loosely. They are conditional on the model's assumptions. The near-term picture is the most robust part; the long-range numbers are best used to understand risks.

The work itself will change regardless. Expect less dictating and measuring, more consulting, procedures and checking AI. AI saves a median of radiologist time by 2045.

Decision points and what to watch

You do not have to decide now. Each step below is a chance to update on new evidence before the next commitment.

  1. 2027–28 · M2
    Read the first large real-world studies of AI-drafted reports. Single-digit time savings support the median forecast.
  2. 2028–29 · M3, specialty choicebiggest update
    Check the signposts on the right. If several have turned pessimistic, the oversupply odds for 2035 are much higher than .
  3. Sep 2029 – Mar 2030 · applications and Match
    Watch the number of DR positions. A jump past ~1,400 adds supply exactly when you graduate.
  4. 2033–34 · R3–R4, fellowship choice
    Tasks that are slower to automate (IR and procedures, breast intervention, consultation-heavy subspecialties, informatics and AI governance) are hedges against the transformative scenario.
  5. 2035 · first job
    Geographic flexibility matters most in surplus futures, which hit new graduates first.
